# AgriXpert Plugin — Public User Guide

This describes what a visitor to the public-facing site can do with the pages a site administrator has built using AgriXpert's shortcodes. It assumes the site owner has already placed the relevant shortcodes on pages (see `docs/INSTALL.md` §3) — exactly which pages exist and what they're called depends on how this particular site is set up.

## Registering as a farmer, buyer, supplier, or cooperative

Each registration form asks for the relevant details for that role (contact information, location, and role-specific fields such as crops grown or products needed), plus an optional set of value-chain interests. All forms require ticking a consent checkbox agreeing to the site's Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Cookie Policy before submitting.

After submitting:
- You'll see a confirmation message on the page.
- Your registration is reviewed by the site's team before being marked verified — this isn't instant.
- You'll receive an email once a decision is made.

If a form shows an error after submitting, the message will say what's wrong (a required field missing, an invalid email, or — if you've submitted several times in quick succession — a request to wait before trying again).

## Contacting the site

The general enquiry/contact form works the same way as the registration forms: fill it in, agree to the policies, and submit. There's no account or login required for this.

## Using the marketplace

The marketplace page lists produce, equipment, and storage space. What you can do depends on whether you're logged in:

- **Browsing listings** (produce, equipment for hire, storage for hire) is open to anyone.
- **Posting a listing**, **expressing interest in one**, or **booking equipment or storage** requires being logged in as a registered farmer, buyer, supplier, or cooperative on the site. If you try one of these while logged out, you'll be prompted to log in first.
- Equipment and storage availability shown on a listing reflects actual current bookings, not a fixed calendar — so what you see is always up to date.

## My Account (the Portal)

If the site has an account/"My Account" page, logging in there (using the same email you registered with — your registration is linked to your account automatically the first time you log in) lets you:

- See whether your registration is still pending, has been verified, or was not approved.
- Update some of your own details (phone number, location, and similar fields) without re-registering or contacting the site's team.
- If you registered a cooperative: add or remove members from your cooperative's roster.
- Leave a rating and comment on an equipment or storage booking once its booking period has ended.

If you don't see your registration there after logging in, make sure you're using the same email address you registered with.

## Insights

If the site has an Insights/articles page, it's a straightforward read-only list of articles published by the site's team — no account needed to view it.

## Your data

Registration details you submit are reviewed by the site's own administrators, who decide whether to verify your registration. This plugin does not share your information with any third party beyond what's needed to send you the confirmation/decision email described above. For specifics, refer to the site's own Privacy Policy page (linked from every registration form).
